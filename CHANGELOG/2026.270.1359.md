- **`rekey-legacy-vm-overlay`** (operator-decided rule, `touches_host: true`): over the per-host overlay's `deploy:` map, a `^vm:` key with a dotted identity twin (`VmDomainIdentity` match) is DROPPED; a lone `^vm:` key HARD-FAILS with the verbatim actionable message (the lossy identity is not recoverable). Only `^vm:` keys are candidates — valid identity keys (`charly.check-k3s-vm`, `versa/ecovoyage`) are untouched.
- **Tests**: idempotency (2nd run = no-op), twin-drop, orphan-error (byte-equality on the message, non-zero exit), nesting round-trip untouched, plus the compact-node-form already-current no-op.
